Three steps. Zero setup.

Works on live sites, staging and localhost.

Install the extension

Add MY Simulator to Chrome, Edge or Brave and pin it to your toolbar.

Open any page

Click the icon or press Alt + Shift + M on the page you want to test.

Pick your devices

Press D for the library, R to rotate, S for a screenshot.

Questions

Is MY Simulator free?

Yes. Every device, screenshot and sync feature is free, with no account required.

Does it emulate the mobile user agent?

Yes. Each frame is loaded with the matching iOS, Android, iPad or TV user agent and touch support, so sites serve their mobile layouts.

Can I test localhost?

Yes. Open your local dev server in Chrome, then open MY Simulator — it works on localhost and staging environments.

Is my browsing data sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Settings are stored in Chrome storage and screenshots are saved directly to your computer.
